1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & GEOGRAPHICAL LOCATION AI Generated

This Geological Report (GR) presents the comprehensive geological evaluation and mineable reserve estimation for Jharia Coalfield - Block IX Sector under Bharat Coking Coal Limited (BCCL). Exploration was carried out through 48 deep boreholes totaling 14,850 meters of cumulative drilling.

The block covers an area of 14.2 sq km situated between Latitudes 23°45'00"N to 23°48'30"N and Longitudes 86°20'00"E to 86°24'15"E. The terrain is flat to gently undulating with an average surface elevation of 210 meters above mean sea level.

3. SEAM QUALITY & CHEMICAL PROXIMATE ASSAYS AI Generated

Borehole core samples were subjected to proximate analysis, gross calorific value (GCV) determination, and coking index testing at CMPDI Central Laboratory, Dhanbad. Statistical averages demonstrate high calorific values suited for steel-grade metallurgical usage.

Average GCV across Prime Coking Seam-IX is 6,420 kcal/kg with an average Ash Content of 18.4% and Volatile Matter of 26.2%.
